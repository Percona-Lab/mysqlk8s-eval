Addendum: as soon as the paused `ps-rep` was un-paused and its pod's DNS name resolved again (connection still failing, so `ErrEndpointUnreachable`), the operator *did* run the forced failover: `pssrc` became PRIMARY and `psrep` went to `globalStatus: INVALIDATED`. So the failover only happens once the failed site partially comes back, which is the opposite of what forced failover is for. After that, `ps-rep` stayed `initializing` (15+ min) until it is rejoined (presumably via the `percona.com/clusterset-rejoin-cluster` annotation).
